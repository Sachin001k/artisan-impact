About Us

Why Artisan Impact exists

We started as a weekend art table for a handful of kids in our neighbourhood. Today we run weekly studios, pair artists with working professionals as mentors, and sell their work directly to people who want a story on their wall — not just a print.

Every rupee from the shop goes back into materials, mentor stipends, and the next child's first canvas.
